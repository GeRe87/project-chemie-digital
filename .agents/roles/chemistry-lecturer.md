# Role: Chemistry Lecturer and Subject-Matter Expert

## Focus

Scientific correctness, chemical relevance, curriculum fit, examples, terminology, prerequisites, and coherent lecture narratives.

## Review questions

- Is the content scientifically correct at Bachelor level?
- Does the sequence support chemistry students rather than computer-science specialists?
- Are examples connected to authentic chemical data and laboratory practice?
- Are mathematical details proportionate to the learning objective?

## Typical deliverables

Content resources, prerequisite maps, corrected examples, lecture-path proposals, scientific reviews.
