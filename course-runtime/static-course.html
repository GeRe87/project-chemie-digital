TED-GC-MS: the complete setup

Thermal decomposition

  • **Controlled heating** under inert gas - **Range:** 25–1000 °C
  • **Polymers** release smaller products
  • ==Matrix compounds can evolve too==

Sorption and transfer

  • **Twister:** captures only a small fraction of evolved compounds
  • **Transfer** the loaded sorbent
  • **Recondition** after measurement for reuse; check blanks

Cryofocusing and injection

  • **Desorb** from the heated sorbent
  • **Cryofocus** in the cooled inlet
  • **Rapid heating** → ==narrow injection==

GC separation → MS detection

Markers → polymer mass

  • **Identify**: retention + ion pattern
  • **Quantify**: peak area + calibration
  • ==Validate for polymer, matrix and sample==

Why TED-GC-MS needs replicates

R^2_{\mathrm{TED}}=0.741\qquad R^2_{\mathrm{UV}}=0.990
R², TED, UV, =
R²: dimensionless squared correlation for a linear fit with intercept, not uncertainty. Subscripts: supplied TED standards 6–10; artificial UV-visible comparison. =: equals; rounded values.
**Plan replicates.** Check prediction and uncertainty. Replicates do not repair bias or invalid calibration.

Measurement time is the bottleneck

  • **6 runs:** 3 samples + 3 blanks
  • **S** = sample · **B** = blank
  • ==Serial total: 630 min (10.5 h)==

Nest the instrument cycles

  • **Overlap** TGA reset and GC-MS
  • **Earlier transfer:** 30 → 20 min
  • ==Batch: 630 → 423 → 338 min==

Optimize heating, preserve reproducibility

  • **SBR marker:** response reproducibility matters
  • ==Lowest sampled value: 15 °C/min==
  • **Credit:** Rajshree Jeewon · Master Thesis, 2026

Our TGA temperature program

  • **Hold:** 200 °C, 2 min; nitrogen 20 mL/min
  • **Ramp comparison:** 10 or 15 K/min to 500 °C
  • **Hold:** 500 °C, 6 min; settling/reset excluded

Calibration still consumes the day

  • **5 standards + 5 blanks**
  • **550 min:** 9 h 10 min
  • ==Even with the optimized schedule==

Balanced running calibration

  • **Cover** the mass range
  • **Update:** oldest out, newest in
  • ==Check held-out prediction, not only fit==

Sample content from local calibration

  • **Local:** one fit per sample
  • **Replicates:** three normalized results per sample
  • ==Whiskers show descriptive standard error, not total uncertainty==

Running calibration: how much does order matter?

  • **5 standards:** selection order matters more
  • **About 15:** similar errors across three replacement strategies
  • ==Robust to order here, not automatically to drift==
